About Our School

For over two decades, St. Ann's Indian High School has nurtured young minds in the heart of Padmanabhanagar, Bangalore, offering a warm, disciplined and joyful place to learn.

Our dedicated teachers treat every child as a unique individual, combining strong academics with sports, values and creativity, so students grow into confident, compassionate and responsible citizens.

With a co-educational environment, well-kept classrooms and a caring community, we partner with parents to help every student reach their fullest potential.
